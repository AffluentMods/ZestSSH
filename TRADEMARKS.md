## What a build you give to others must do

A build you distribute, changed or not, is yours and must look like yours:

- **its own name and icon**, not confusingly similar to ZestSSH's;
- **its own application id** on every platform (Android application id, iOS and macOS bundle id, Linux application
  id, Windows installer id and Store identity), its own data folders and its own link scheme, so that it never opens
  or replaces the official app's data or answers its links;
- **its own servers and update channel**: it must not talk to api.zestssh.com, sign people in to ZestSSH accounts,
  or update itself from zestssh.com;
- **its own privacy policy, terms and support contact**: it must not show ours as if they covered it;
- a plain statement that it is not made or endorsed by Affluent Labs LLC.
